# Merged finding claims

Choice: When sightings or findings merge, the finding that speaks keeps its own explanation, failure scenario, and evidence. The strongest cause still wins, and the member that proved it adds its `cause` locations to the speaker's evidence, ten locations in all, so the cause travels with its proof. Every other member's failure scenario and evidence are kept beside the speaker's, per member, in `otherClaims`. Nothing is dropped, and nothing lowers.

Why: The first rule moved the proving member's scenario and evidence onto the speaker and dropped the speaker's own, so a merged finding paired one lens's explanation with another lens's proof, and a verifier would judge a claim nobody made. Keeping each claim whole lets the verifier judge each on its own evidence, while the imported `cause` locations keep a merged blocker from losing the proof that makes it block.
