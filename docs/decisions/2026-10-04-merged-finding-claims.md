# Merged finding claims

Choice: When sightings or findings merge, the finding that speaks keeps its own explanation, failure scenario, and evidence. The strongest cause still wins, and the member that proved it adds its `cause` locations to the speaker's evidence, ten locations in all, those that overlap the change first, so the cause travels with its proof. `report_finding` marks each `cause` location that overlaps the change `proves: true` when it classifies the finding; a merged finding is `affected` only through a `cause` location so marked, or, for a finding stored before the mark existed, through any `cause` location. Every other member's failure scenario and evidence are kept beside the speaker's, per member, in `otherClaims`. Nothing is dropped, and nothing lowers.

Why: The first rule moved the proving member's scenario and evidence onto the speaker and dropped the speaker's own, so a merged finding paired one lens's explanation with another lens's proof, and a verifier would judge a claim nobody made. Keeping each claim whole lets the verifier judge each on its own evidence, while the imported `cause` locations keep a merged blocker from losing the proof that makes it block. A lens may mark more than one location `cause`, the defect's own unchanged line among them, and the merge cannot see the change, so without the mark a full speaker kept a `cause` location that proved nothing and dropped the one that did.
