# Cache scratch recovery

SIGKILL skips finally and leaves fetch, graph and coverage scratch. New temporary names include the process ID. Every cache open scans its tools, graphs and coverage roots to remove scratch whose process no longer exists. A live process or denied liveness probe preserves it. The sweep never follows symlinks or enters published tool entries. Legacy scratch without an owner waits one day, avoiding deletion of an older build’s active writer. This extends the worktree recovery policy to disposable cache files.
