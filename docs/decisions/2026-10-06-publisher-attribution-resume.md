# Attribution when publication resumes

Supersedes: 2026-10-06-writer-trust-status.md.

Publication keeps the writer trust and author rules of the earlier decision. Before resuming an interrupted task, it records the full current publisher attribution. A changed trust setting or previously known login, publisher permission or author permission supersedes the old task before any write. Unknown legacy fields do not invent an identity or prevent migration.

A status-crash regression switches from a writer to another login with maintain permission. The new task posts one review and one ledger and records the current attribution.
