# Milestone order

Choice: Milestone 2 becomes "Melian is the review of record for Melian", milestone 3 "Melian reviews pull requests on GitHub Actions" (the Actions host, the state branch, container isolation, the credential pool, Opengrep, and gitleaks), and milestone 4 "Melian remembers and learns" (comment commands, knowledge write-back, the decision-model adapters, and calibration). The milestones are renumbered, not reordered in place. Milestone 2 takes `melian dismiss`, the verification pass with its LLM executor, triage at intake through the `Decider` port with the LLM fallback adapter, and the tool manifest with Enola as its first tool.

Why: Authority over the Melian repository needs lens coverage, a verifier, dismissal, and a required status check, and none of them needs the Actions host. The classification of 151 accepted findings from the comparison records ([research/2026-10-04-review-findings-by-bucket.md](../research/2026-10-04-review-findings-by-bucket.md)) shows correctness, at 54, is the only bucket today's lenses plausibly cover. Trust boundary, at 28, and durability, at 17, the high-severity buckets, have no check at all, and only about 11 of the 151 could be a static rule.
