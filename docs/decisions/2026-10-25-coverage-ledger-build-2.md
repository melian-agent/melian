# Delta rounds, the carry and the coverage gate settle six questions the design left to the build

Amends: [the coverage decision](2026-10-23-coverage-ledger-and-delta-review.md), its build 2: when a round is a delta, the level a delta lens runs at, the prompt every round renders, what review.coverage says, where the carry puts a changed finding, and how a gap dismissal takes effect

For plan step 24, build 2, after [the build 1 decision](2026-10-25-coverage-ledger-build-1.md). It builds delta rounds, the carry of a decided round's lens findings, `review.coverage`, and the maintainer's dismissal of a gap. Each choice below is stricter than, or equal to, what the decision allows. [Melian's flow]

## Choices

1. **A round is a delta when coverage carries to a new head.** At intake the ledger maps the last decided round's coverage to the head. When any lens coverage carries and the head moved, the round is a delta: triage does not run, and each lens reviews what it has left. A repeat review of the same base and head is no delta. It runs the same selection as before, so it attaches to the round that decided the head and spends nothing, as the decision's repeat review asks. A round with nothing carried, after a void that takes everything or under `--cold`, reviews the whole diff with triage, as today.
2. **A delta lens runs at the higher of two levels.** One is the level the last decided round's own run reached, and the other is its default within its band, bounded by the band and its routed levels.
3. **Every round renders the new prompt.** Each file block's second line names both modes and kinds, whether the file is binary, a symlink's target up to 4 KiB, and whether the file is empty. Blocks go smallest first, and a block that does not fit is left out while smaller ones still go in. The prompt lists every hunk it left out by its lines. File units it left out get a metadata block, under 64 KiB. The change changes every lens's instruction fingerprint once, so the first review after the upgrade runs its lenses again rather than attach to a task an older build created.
4. **review.coverage points at a check that did not run.** When the gap is a check whose own record did not run, the verdict already names that check, so review.coverage's reason names it and says its record did not cover the diff, rather than listing every line. Other gaps are listed, five at most, with a count of the rest.
5. **A changed finding sits after its hunk, and its cause is classified again.** The carry places a finding whose cited lines a hunk changed at the first new line after that hunk, as the decision says, and classifies its cause against the whole diff there. That line is often base code, so such a finding often reads pre-existing until its lens restates it. A finding in a file the move deleted is not carried; its lens reviews the whole diff again, so it is judged rather than lost.
6. **A gap dismissal takes effect at the next review.** `melian dismiss-gap` records the dismissal in the store, beside the round it answers, and changes no verdict at once. The next `melian review` of the head decides the verdict with it: its `review.coverage` reads the dismissal and the review attaches to every lens it ran, so it spends no model. A dismissal carries to a later head when the move leaves its named lines alone, and is reopened when any of them changes.

After the upgrade, a repeat review of a head decided before it mints one more round, since every manifest now names `review.coverage`. It runs no lens.
