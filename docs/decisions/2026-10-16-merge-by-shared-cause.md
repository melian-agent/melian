# Reports of one defect merge when one hunk caused them

Choice: two findings from different checks, in the same file, with overlapping lines at head, are one defect when the change caused both through the same hunk. A finding falls on a hunk when its trigger names that hunk, or when one of its `cause` locations falls on it through `Revision.changeOverlap`: a head cause on the hunk's new lines, a base cause on its old lines. A finding joins a group only when it meets every member under the rule, so merges never chain, and the groups do not depend on the input order. The snippet rule stays as a second way in. A `pre-existing` finding falls on no hunk, so it merges only by its snippet, as before. Every other rule of the merge stands: findings from one check never merge, only findings with the same lifecycle status merge, `ruleAliases` with `distinct: true` keeps two rules apart, the speaker takes the highest severity and the strongest cause, and `mergeClaims` keeps every claim. Melian's fixed lens instructions gain one sentence: put a finding on the line or lines that break, and cite any wider span as evidence.

Why: the merge depended on which lines a model chose to cite. The snippet is the head's text at the finding's lines, so two lenses that cite different ranges for one defect have different snippets, and the "no fuzzy matching" rule kept them apart. On seven design goldens, Opus finders left one fail-open default in `src/publish.ts` as two to four findings: the design lens's `fail-open-default` at lines 6-7, the trust-boundary lens's `fail-open` at lines 5-7, and the removed-behaviour lens's `dropped-guard` at lines 3-8. Sol finders cited line 6 alone in every lens, so the same reports merged and precision was 1.00. [The cross-cells record](../../packages/evals/runs/2026-10-16-finder-verifier-cross-cells.md) holds the runs. One hunk changed those lines, and every report traces to it. The `dropped-guard` report cites its cause at the base, `src/publish.ts:3-6 at base`, the required option the hunk replaced, and the head lines only as context; a rule on head causes alone would have left it apart. The hunk comes from the diff, not from the model's wording, so the merge stays mechanical. A first draft of this rule asked for overlapping `cause` locations as well, but lenses cite their own lines as `cause`, so that condition added almost nothing and missed the base cause. The instruction sentence narrows the finding's own range, so the snippet rule catches more of the same cases on its own.

No chaining. Problem: a finding that met any one member joined the group. Example: reports at lines 1-5, 5-7 and 7-8 on one hunk became one defect, though the first and last share no line. Solution: a finding must meet every member, so they stay at least two defects.

The accepted cost: two distinct defects on one hunk, over overlapping lines, now merge. Example: a cleanup lens reports a P3 on line 6 of `canPublish`, and the design lens a P0 spanning lines 3-8, both through the one hunk. They become one finding, and the P0 speaks. Three things protect the author:

- the speaker lists the P3 in `alsoReportedAs`, so the review shows both rules;
- `melian dismiss` prints "Also dismissed, as reports adjudication merged into it:" with each merged report's rule, check and ID, and `--only` dismisses one report alone;
- `ruleAliases` with `distinct: true` keeps two rules apart, in either direction.

A finding in another file still does not merge, even when the same hunk caused it. Example: on the same goldens, the correctness lens reports the caller at `src/cli.ts:8` and cites `src/publish.ts:5-7` as its cause. It stays its own finding. That is accepted: the caller is where a second fix may be needed, since `republishCommand` must pass the policy's answer whatever `canPublish` defaults to, and an author reading the review reads each file's comments where they sit. Merging across files would also move a finding off the lines its comment is posted on.

The instruction sentence changes every lens's version hash, since the fixed block is part of each lens's rendered instructions. The pinned versions and golden snapshots are updated in the same change, deliberately, and a sighting stored under an older version stays a sighting of that version.

No earlier decision file records the merge rule; [docs/guidelines/core.md](../guidelines/core.md#dedupe-across-sources) and [design.md](../design.md#the-pipeline) set it out, and both change with this one.
