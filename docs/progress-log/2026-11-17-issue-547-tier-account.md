[Issue #547](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/547) moved the rule that decides which skipped checks still let a review pass out of `account()` in the review and into `TierAccount` in core, as [the decision](../decisions/2026-11-17-tier-account-decides-allowed-skips.md) records. `TierAccount.of(config, tier, lenses)` fills the records for the tier's unrecorded lenses and questions, derives `allowSkip` from the round's own facts, and its `refusal` names how an account differs from that, with a code from a closed set and no record text. Tests pinned the review's account for each of the five reasons a skip passes before the rule moved, and they pass unchanged after it. A further test holds that the default floor is `careful` or `quick` whatever the head's trust and the decider's calibration, so only a configured floor of `skip` lets triage skip a lens. The record relay of [issue #397](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/397) and the record reader of [issue #395](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/395) will call `refusal`.
