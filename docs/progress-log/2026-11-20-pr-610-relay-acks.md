# The relay's three acknowledges

[Issue #610](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/610) fixes the three acknowledge findings that the host round at 02d0ad4c raised on [pull request #576](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/576). The maintainer ruled on 2026-11-20, in the coordinator's session, that #576 lands first and these follow before the gate. Each fix starts from a test that fails at the head before it.

- The relay counted triage records against its bound of 64 only when the round did not name them, so a round that named every triage record could hand over any number. 6b3467d8 counts triage, the round's telemetry and early lens copies whether or not the round names them. The new test takes 64 named triage records and refuses 65; it failed before the fix.
- A writer's batch could hold two triage records with decisions, and the account read whichever came first. b60efc93 refuses a second decided triage record with `triageCount`. Three tests failed before the fix: two decisions that disagree, two that agree, and a second decision the account does not count.
- No test held the relay to the approved base's tier once `main` moved and both commits trust writers. e51addfa4 adds one in which the tip adds `static.tsc`, with and without `--check`. Both cases pass at the head and fail when the relay reads its tier from the base branch's tip.
