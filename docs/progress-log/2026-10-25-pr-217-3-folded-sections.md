[Pull request #217](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/217) folds the review body's sections that ask nothing of the reader, as the maintainer ruled on 2026-10-25. Advisories fold under "N advisories" and attested dismissals under "N dismissed, M reasons", while blocking and acknowledgement findings, the verdict and the checks that did not run stay open. Inside the dismissals, a reason that several dismissals share is said once, above the findings it covers; [pull request #202](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/202)'s review had listed one reason eight times. [The decision](../decisions/2026-10-25-lens-notes-leave-the-review-body.md) records the rule.
