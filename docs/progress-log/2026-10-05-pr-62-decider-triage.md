Triage chooses each lens's level, in [pull request #62](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/62), which closes milestone 2 step 5 and [issue #24](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/24). Core gains the `Decider` port, a `Decision` that keeps the whole distribution, `LevelBand`, and `EscalationRule`, and a lens asks its own triage question and takes its level within its band. `packages/decisions` holds the recorded adapter and the LLM fallback, which asks a text model through core's `TextModel` port. The pipeline asks triage in a decision task, runs each lens at its level, and reruns a lens at `quick` at its next level when it reports at or above `triage.escalateAt` or a budget ends it before it reports anything. The escalated run stands for the lens, and a quick finding at or above `escalateAt` that it neither restates nor refutes stays counted, attributed to the quick run; the level joins every key that named a lens by `name@version`. `melian review` triages on the cheapest routed tier and refuses a configured `decisions.provider`, and the review records each `decisions.*` check itself. Without a decider every lens still runs at `careful`. [The decision](../decisions/2026-10-05-triage-as-built.md) records the choices the design left open.
