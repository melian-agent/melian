# Lenses report every real defect they meet, on a severity scale

Builder agent, for [issue #655](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/655). Six built-in lenses, `contracts`, `correctness`, `design`, `removed-behaviour`, `tests` and `trust-boundary`, now review as the review of record does. Each first learns what the change promises, then attacks each promise, and reports every real defect it meets: under its own rules, or under the new `melian/other-defect`, which the review adds to every lens beside `melian/injection-attempt`. Hand-offs describe a neighbour's focus, and no longer tell a lens to leave a defect to it. The shared instructions in `packages/core/src/lens.ts` add one severity scale, read by what happens once the change is merged. The verifier may lower by one step a finding its own scenario does not support, and a document that describes a behaviour no longer refutes a claim that the behaviour breaks something. The coordinator's uncommitted experiment, on five merged pull requests, raised the share of reviewers' defects Melian found from 35% to 50% at the same count of blocking findings; [the decision](../decisions/2026-11-28-lenses-report-every-real-defect.md) records the numbers. Applying the experiment's diff to today's `main` kept the lens rules it had dropped by accident, those that were not hold-back sentences: `contracts` reporting the broken dependant's location, `design`'s author standard and report-once rule, `tests`' fake and untested-module rules, and `trust-boundary`'s supply-chain sentence. No findings budget, route, tier or level changed. No key changed shape. Lens versions, the verifier's version and the request keys moved with their text, so the first round after this lands asks every lens and the verifier again once.
