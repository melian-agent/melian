[Pull request #16](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/16) brought up to date with the review fixes of [pull request #15](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/15). Adjudication reads findings as sightings through `readFindings` with the lenses the review ran, so a disabled or retiered lens's sightings leave the verdict as they leave `Review.findings`; a finding without a resolution is not yet adjudicated, never silent, and the verdict's findings are typed `ResolvedFinding`; a lens whose route runs out becomes a failed check; and a repeat review of a head attaches to its adjudication task through the review index, with a superseded task recording no verdict.
