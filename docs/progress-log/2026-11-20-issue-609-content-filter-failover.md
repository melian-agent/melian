# A content filter's refusal fails a lens over to its next model

[Issue #609](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/609). When a provider's content filter refuses a lens, the lens now moves to its tier's next accepted model, as it does on a usage limit, by the maintainer's ruling of 2026-11-20. Codex had refused the probing lens's planner on [pull request #532](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/532) and [pull request #576](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/576), and each round read not reviewed. `isContentRefusal` in `packages/pipeline/src/harness.ts` matches the whole message each of pi-ai's adapters builds for a refusal, and `isFailoverError` now counts it. The lens task reads each earlier attempt's settled submission, so the lens's record names the models that refused and the model that answered, for the lens and for its prober. A lens whose every model refused fails, and its reason names each. The planner, the prober and the verifier fail over. Triage and the walkthrough fail over on nothing, as before. [The decision](../decisions/2026-11-20-content-filter-refusal-fails-over.md) lists the matched messages and the ones left out.

The tests were written first. Before the fix, the planner test failed with `ReviewError: lenses did not finish: trust-boundary`, and the all-refused test with `expected 'lensFailed' to be 'allModelsFailed'`. After the fix, both pass. `harness-failover.test.ts` covers each matched message and each near miss: a prefix that comes later in the message, a fixed message with more text after it, a limit, Gemini's `RECITATION` and Bedrock's `guardrail_intervened`.

`harness.ts` and `review.ts` guard trust: they decide which model reviews a change and what a lens's record says of it. [The inventory](2026-11-20-issue-609-mutants.json), from `melian mutants list origin/main...HEAD --claims-in` for those two files at `e941d2caa`, lists 97 mutants: 79 generated and 18 ignored by configuration. A scratch runner applied each generated mutant and ran its named test alone, after that test passed unmutated. The results:

- 71 killed by an existing test. Two of these, `at >= attempt` and `at--` in the loop over earlier attempts, never end, so the named test hit its timeout.
- 4 killed by two tests added after the first run. These seeded a refusal into the reason of a lens that had none.
- 4 equivalent. Each survived the whole pipeline suite of 3,974 tests, and each carries its reason in the inventory.
