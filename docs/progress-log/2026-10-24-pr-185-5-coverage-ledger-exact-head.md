# 2026-10-24: the coverage ledger takes mutation evidence at the exact head and lets no notice cover a hunk

Codex's adversarial review of [pull request #185](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/185) at `577a0750` found two holes. First, the ledger accepted a verification record from an earlier head when the mutant's line mapped unchanged, yet a change to `b.ts` can make an earlier-killed mutant in unchanged `a.ts` survive. [The decision](../decisions/2026-10-23-coverage-ledger-and-delta-review.md) now adopts the contract of [pull request #189](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/189): a record covers a mutant only when its head, its adapter and installation identities, and a digest of the effective base-sourced mutation policy with the resolved test-command argv all match. Mutation coverage never maps or carries. Settled survivor findings, dismissals, `invalid` notes and `ignored` mutants cover as #189 says, and an `invalid-claim` record covers nothing. Voids 7 and 8 shrink for mutation evidence to "a record from another head is never evidence", and a new void 11 covers a base whose mutation policy changes. The `Amends:` line now names #189's decision instead of the rerun decision. Second, a policy-change notice no longer covers an oversized analyser-configuration or lockfile hunk. Such a hunk reaches each lens in bounded chunks through `read_file`, and stays a gap until that lens's reads deliver every line; the one escape is a maintainer's dismissal naming the check and the lines, recorded as a dismissal. Attack rules 15 and 16 state that `review.coverage` cannot pass on a stale kill or on an oversized hunk no lens received, and the plan's tests say the same.
