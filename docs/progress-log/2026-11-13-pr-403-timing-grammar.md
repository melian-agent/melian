The `Timing:` line that ends every builder report now has a fixed form, for [issue #401](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/401). The maintainer asked for it on 2026-11-13, after a rollup of 20 merged pull requests found most lines could not be totalled. AGENTS.md's Work from issues gives the grammar: items of a closed list of kinds, each with a count, minutes, an optional cost, a wait's cause and an optional note. `scripts/timeline.mjs` parses it, totals minutes and cost per kind, and sums the summary's total from those rows, so the two cannot disagree. Every item it cannot parse is listed under its own heading and counted nowhere. The old parser half-counted such items: it read "gate 4 × about 13 min" as 13 minutes under the label "gate 4 × about". On [pull request #387](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/387) the reported time falls from 152 to 109 minutes, and six items are listed as unparsed. Older lines still read where they begin with a known kind or `waited N min for`. The rollup's unparsed lines sit in `scripts/fixtures/pr-timeline/rollup-unparsed.json`, each beside its nearest legal form.
