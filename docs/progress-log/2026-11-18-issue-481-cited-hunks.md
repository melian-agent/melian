# Issue #481: a shared reader remembers every cited hunk

Codex's adversarial review of [pull request #575](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/575) found that a round read from shared records could keep a dismissal the host had reopened. The host remembers every proving hunk later sightings of a dismissed finding cite; the reader met a dismissal against the record's proof and the current sighting's alone. The coordinator answered on 2026-11-18: work that memory out from the records the reader already lists, with no change to any record's shape. That is a coordinator's answer, not a ruling. [The decision](../decisions/2026-11-18-a-shared-reader-remembers-every-cited-hunk.md) records the rule and its limit.

The test came first. `findings.test.ts` drives the host through three revisions: dismissed citing A, then citing A and B, then B changed and citing A. On the head before the fix the host read `new` and the reader `dismissed`: `expected [ 'dismissed' ] to deeply equal [ 'new' ]`. `recorded-round.test.ts` pins it through records, and failed there with `expected [ 'passed', false ] to deeply equal [ 'findings', true ]`. Both pass with the fix, and each pins the limit: without the second revision's round, the reader keeps the dismissal.

Mutants of `findings.ts` and `recorded-round.ts`, enumerated with `melian mutants list 539f9cda7...HEAD --claims-in` both files `--json --all`, ran by hand at 9dc6915bc: 45 of 45 killed, each by one named test run alone, which passed unmutated and failed mutated. The first run, at 37fb82f50, killed 42 of 50. Its survivors were the changeset guard, the `"check"` kind and the fallback for a finding no round sights, each now killed by a test of its own. The other three were in the branch that gathered a dismissal record's own sightings, which 9dc6915bc removed, since a dismissal's trigger proof already holds every hunk its sighting cites.

Timing: gate 2 × 15 min; codex 1 × 12 min; mutants 2 × 40 min; other 1 × 20 min (container restart: reran the gate and 17 mutants).

Codex's second review, at 6a06c0449, found that the reader kept the memory for the finding, for all time, so a dismissal made again after a reopening could never hold, and a lens record no round names poisoned the finding the same way. The coordinator corrected the rule on 2026-11-18: the memory belongs to a standing dismissal and starts at it, and only records a counting round names, at rounds since the dismissal, count. That is a coordinator's answer, not a ruling. The tests came first: on 6a06c0449 the record no round names, the dismissal made again, the dismissal at the revision read, and two of the three broken chains all failed on the dismissal's status, while the first reopening passed. All pass with the fix.
