The maintainer adopted [the coordination decision](../decisions/2026-11-08-how-melian-coordinates.md): one mechanism for each kind of collision. Within a process, a Pi Durable commit; across processes and hosts, core's `Lease` with a fencing generation; for writes to GitHub, idempotent and re-checked writes; and for lost or reordered events, reconciliation to the authority. `docs/design.md` gains a short section. [Pull request #310](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/310) reshapes its lease to the shared type. Two follow-ups carry the rest: publication stamping `melian/review` with the lease generation, and the static checks' worktree lock moving onto `Lease`.
