[Issue #502](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/502): a round read back from exported records was decided under whatever resolution policy the reader held, not the one the review used. A change to `melian.yaml` between the review and the read could turn a blocking round into a passing one.

The account record now names the resolution policy fingerprint the review decided under, `resolutionPolicy`, or `null` when the review read no policy source. The fingerprint is part of the account's key, so two reviews of one range under two policies write two accounts. `accountKey` in `packages/pipeline/src/review-records.ts` builds that key for the writer and the reader alike. `RecordedRound.verdict` computes the reader's fingerprint with `configsFor`, as `dismiss` does. It reads not reviewed, naming both fingerprints, when the two differ: a changed policy, a policy on one side only, or none on the other. When they agree it decides as the review did. The account's payload stays at version 1, because nothing on `main` writes an account yet; [the account record decision](../decisions/2026-11-16-account-record.md) lands with [pull request #476](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/476). [The new decision](../decisions/2026-11-16-account-names-its-resolution-policy.md) amends its "where the reader takes its policy".

One existing test, "resolves each finding under its own path's policy when a policy source is given", reviewed with no policy and read under one, so it asserted the behaviour this issue refuses. It now reviews and reads under one source.
