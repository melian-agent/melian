[Issue #502](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/502): a round read back from exported records was decided under whatever resolution policy the reader held, not the one the review used. A change to `melian.yaml` between the review and the read could turn a blocking round into a passing one.

The account record now names the resolution policy fingerprint the review decided under, `resolutionPolicy`, or `null` when the review read no policy source. The fingerprint is part of the account's key, so two reviews of one range under two policies write two accounts. `accountKey` in `packages/pipeline/src/review-records.ts` builds that key for the writer and the reader alike. `RecordedRound.verdict` computes the reader's fingerprint with `configsFor`, as `dismiss` does. It reads not reviewed, naming both fingerprints, when the two differ. When they agree it decides as the review did. [The new decision](../decisions/2026-11-16-account-names-its-resolution-policy.md) amends [the account record decision](../decisions/2026-11-16-account-record.md)'s "where the reader takes its policy".

One existing test, "resolves each finding under its own path's policy when a policy source is given", reviewed with no policy and read under one, so it asserted the behaviour this issue refuses. It now reviews and reads under one source.

Seventh pass, by the maintainer's ruling of 2026-11-17 in the coordinator's session, [recorded on pull request #525](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/525#issuecomment-6928816633). The gate at `1b63c6a6` failed one test, `caveats-reopen.test.ts`, which opens a store `main` wrote and dismisses a finding there. The adjudication task refused it with "the resolution policy changed since the review read it": the policy came from the base commit and had not changed, but the earlier passes changed how the fingerprint is computed. The fingerprint now carries `fingerprintVersion`, `3:`. A review whose policy came from a commit and whose stored fingerprint names an older version decides a dismissal again under the current computation. A working-tree policy, a differing fingerprint of the current version, and a fingerprint with no version are still refused. [The decision](../decisions/2026-11-17-dismissal-decides-again-after-the-fingerprint-changes.md) records the rule.
