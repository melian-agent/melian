# Maintainer questions go through the coordinator, and the coordinator's dismissal authority is recorded

Two decisions on the docs branch of [pull request #508](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/508).

[Builders bring every question for the maintainer through the coordinator](../decisions/2026-11-17-questions-for-the-maintainer-go-through-the-coordinator.md) amends the 30-minute fallback of [the earlier decision](../decisions/2026-11-13-builders-ask-the-coordinator-first.md). `AGENTS.md` no longer sends a builder to the maintainer in its own session for the fallback, the read of acknowledge findings or the cloud-session learning.

[The coordinator's standing authority to dismiss](../decisions/2026-11-17-coordinators-standing-dismissal-authority.md) records a ruling of 2026-10-17, extended on 2026-10-22, that no file held. `AGENTS.md` links it where it speaks of dismissals.
