[Issue #493](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/493): a prober can close a probe it wrote wrongly, by naming it with a reason. The maintainer ruled on 2026-11-18, on [pull request #532](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/532#issuecomment-6964725749), and [the decision](../decisions/2026-11-18-a-wrongly-written-probe-is-closed-with-a-reason.md) amends [the decision of 2026-11-17](../decisions/2026-11-17-a-failed-probe-needs-a-finding.md). Problem: a probe that failed on its own syntax error left its attack unanswered, so the lens ended with no honest way to finish. Solution: `skip_attack` takes `probe`, and accepts it only for a probe that ran in the prober's conversation, probed that attack and failed. The reason goes in the probing document's `closed`, by probe ID, and the lens's record shows it. An attack with a failed probe is answered when a finding rests on one of its failed probes, or when every one of them is closed. The trust floor is unchanged.

The same pass fixes R1 from the review of record at `65a8ccd7`: a lens left with an unanswered attack whose findings budget also ran out now names both in its reason.
