[Pull request #70](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/70) closes a gap in the `overlong-sentence` guardrail from [pull request #66](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/66). The pattern ended its word count at a semicolon or colon, so a sentence of more than 100 words joined by semicolons passed, and the conventions lens had to flag it. A semicolon or colon no longer resets the count; a full stop, question mark, exclamation mark, or table bar still does. The single pattern compiles under the engine's step cap, so no second rule was needed. The rule now fires on 84 existing lines under `docs/`, the comparison records, and `README.md`, up from 37. The rule is advisory, so none blocks a merge. The scripted golden, its `melian.golden.yaml` copy, the `core.md` paragraph, and the tests in `packages/core/test/doc-guardrails.test.ts` follow the rule.
