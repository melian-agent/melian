Each order the plan states is a "blocked by" relationship.
- The open issues are triaged: 23 to milestone 2, 6 to milestone 3, and five with neither.
- The labels `status: ready`, `status: in progress` and `plan step` are new. Issues with no open blocker and a full brief are `status: ready`; the four step 24 builds in review, and #204, #212 and #213, are `status: in progress`.
