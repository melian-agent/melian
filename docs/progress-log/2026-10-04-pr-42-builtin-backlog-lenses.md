Four lenses from the backlog ship built in, in [pull request #42](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/42), the lenses half of milestone 2 step 3: `trust-boundary`, `removed-behaviour`, `tests`, and `conventions`, each adversarial, each with its own rules, the three levels, and `standards: true`, and each in the default `full` tier. [The design](../design.md#the-lens-backlog) says what each covers, and [the boundaries decision](../decisions/2026-10-04-lens-backlog-boundaries.md) records which lens owns a defect two could report, stated on both sides, `correctness` included. Each lens has five goldens drawn from accepted findings in the comparison records, one of them clean, twenty in all, each with a README naming its record and finding, and [goldens/BACKLOG.md](../../packages/evals/goldens/BACKLOG.md) lists the records' other goldens by lens. `correctness-deleted-guard` and `removed-behaviour-dropped-guard` each expect a finding from both `correctness` and `removed-behaviour`. Tests that script only `correctness` and `contracts` pin the tier to those two, and `melian doctor` lists the lenses an unrouted tier blocks as a series. [The sixth live run](../../packages/evals/runs/2026-10-04-live-goldens-6.md) measured the lenses three times as written, found every seeded defect but drew most of them two or three times, tuned the boundaries once, and measured three more passes: corpus precision rose from 0.49 to 0.81 with recall at 0.96, `removed-behaviour` and `tests` meet the bars on their goldens, and `trust-boundary` and `conventions` remain below the precision bar, for the reasons the run names. `durability`, the fifth, follows as a repository lens.
