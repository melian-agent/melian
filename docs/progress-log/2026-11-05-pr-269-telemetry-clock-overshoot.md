[Pull request #269](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/269) caps a conversation stage's end at its owning task's terminal commit, closing [issue #259](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/259). The span-join test in `packages/pipeline/test/stage-telemetry.test.ts` failed three times on Node 22, with `adjudication.prepare` overlapping the verifier's stage. The cause was not touching spans, which the assertion already allowed. `TelemetryReader` ended a conversation at its last reply's wall-clock `timestamp` plus pi-ai's `durationMs`, which is monotonic, measured from the stream's creation and rounded. The sum counts the time before the reply was built twice, so it could pass the task's `endedAt`; in the test's own runs, the margin to the next host step was 1 to 2 ms. A new test fixes the reply's clock values at offsets -1, 0, 1 and 40 from the task's end, and failed at offset 1 before the fix. Of the guard's 13 mutants, 9 are killed by that test and 4 are equivalent.
