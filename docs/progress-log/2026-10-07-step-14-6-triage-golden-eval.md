# Step 14, the triage question-set golden eval

`packages/evals/triage/` holds six changes with a known right level for the `correctness` and `trust-boundary` lenses. `runTriageGolden` reviews each under the real `FallbackDecider`, scripted in the gate and live on `MELIAN_EVAL_TRIAGE=1`, and reads the choice from the stored decision. `TriageResults` scores exact share, mean distance, and under-looks, and `compare` says whether a later measurement chooses better. A gate test holds `triageQuestionSet.version` to a hash of the shipped questions recorded in `questions.json`, so a changed question cannot ship unversioned. A [decision](../decisions/2026-10-07-triage-questions-are-versioned-and-measured.md) records the rule. The right levels are my judgement for the maintainer to confirm.
