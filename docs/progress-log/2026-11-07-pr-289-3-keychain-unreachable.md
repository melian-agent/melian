The keychain is unreachable from a probe, as the maintainer tested by hand on 2026-11-07 for [pull request #289](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/289). sandbox-runtime 0.0.81 lets a probe look up `com.apple.SecurityServer` and `com.apple.securityd.xpc`, and offers no setting that denies them, so the question was what a probe could ask them. He ran `melian probe` from the branch at bad33202 on his Mac, against two canaries in his login keychain, a generic and an internet password added with `-A`, which `security` read outside the sandbox. Inside the probe, `find-generic-password` and `find-internet-password` each answered "SecKeychainSearchCopyNext: The specified item could not be found", through the default search list and with `/Users/tal/Library/Keychains/login.keychain-db` named by its path. `dump-keychain` on that path returned no canary, and `list-keychains` answered. Ruling 1 is settled and macOS probes stay on; [the decision](../decisions/2026-11-07-probes-run-under-sandbox-runtime.md) records the evidence, and `packages/pipeline/test/probe-keychain.test.ts` repeats it on CI's macOS runner against a throwaway keychain.
