[Pull request #465](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/465) makes core the one test of a changeset ID's shape, for [issue #461](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/461). Store prune and the CLI each carried their own regex for `pull-` or `range-` and sixteen lowercase hex digits. A change to the length core builds would have left prune listing every real store as not recognised, and the CLI refusing every real round ID, with no test failing. Core now exports `isChangesetId` beside `pullRequestChangesetId` and `Changeset.resolve`, and both builders take their length from the constant the test reads. `store-prune.ts` and `comparison-stores.ts` call it after stripping their own `.sqlite` suffixes. `compare.ts` and `commands.ts` call it through `roundChangeset` in `packages/cli/src/repository.ts`, which strips a round's `.r<n>` or `.k<hex>`. What each caller accepts is unchanged, and the [identity guideline](../guidelines/identity.md#the-keys-melian-has) names the test in both changeset ID rows. The IDs do not change, so no stored data moves. The core test failed 9 of 9 at a36d73cb, with `TypeError: undefined is not a function` because core exported no such function, and passes 9 of 9 after the change.
