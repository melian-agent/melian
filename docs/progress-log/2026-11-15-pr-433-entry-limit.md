The maintainer ruled on 2026-11-15, in the coordinator's session, on three limits the second Codex review of [pull request #433](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/433) raised: build the entry limit now, and accept two others until their issues.

A call held every entry of the tip in memory, and git's listing of them, with no bound. Since a0a42409, a tip of more than 65,536 entries fails the call as `unreachable` before any size is read, and the listing stops at 64 MiB, a KiB a line on average, failing the same way. Four tests sit at each bound and one past it. On 65de1b99 the two past the bounds failed, each because the call listed the tip and answered: `promise resolved "[ 'noise/0', 'noise/1', …(65535) ]" instead of rejecting`. On a0a42409 the suite's 150 tests pass. The test fixture now hashes each distinct content once, so a tip of 65,537 entries plants in seconds.

[Its inventory](2026-11-15-pr-433-entry-limit-mutants.json) runs 26 mutants by hand at a0a42409, each against one named test that passed unmutated first, and each fails it. Twenty-two are Stryker's. Four change the limits' numbers by one, which Stryker never does: 65,535 and 65,537 entries, and 1,023 and 1,025 bytes a line.

[The decision](../decisions/2026-11-14-git-record-store-refuses-by-location.md#what-one-call-uses-and-what-bounds-it) now lists every resource a call takes in a table, with what bounds it or who accepted it unbounded. A handle's cache of judged entries waits for [issue #455](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/455). What a killed process leaves behind waits for [issue #460](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/460), which says what makes a leftover provably stale. The table also records one gap the builder found while writing it: core's `git` keeps a command's stderr whole, and a fetch's stderr carries what the remote sends.
