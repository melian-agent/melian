Milestone 2 was reshaped in [pull request #31](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/31) around Melian becoming the review of record for its own repository. Authority over the repository needs lens coverage, a verifier, dismissal, and a required status check, and none of them needs the Actions host, so the Actions host moves to milestone 3 and learning to milestone 4. [The classification of 151 accepted findings](../research/2026-10-04-review-findings-by-bucket.md) set the order: correctness is the only bucket today's lenses plausibly cover, and trust boundary and durability, the high-severity buckets, have no check at all. A new [research/](../research/) directory holds that note and three more: [a comparison of review tools](../research/2026-10-04-review-tools-compared.md), [Enola](../research/2026-10-04-enola.md), and [the anatomy of review output](../research/2026-10-04-review-output-anatomy.md). [design.md](../design.md) and the [plan](../design-implementation-plan.md) state the decisions, recorded in [milestone order](../decisions/2026-10-04-milestone-order.md), [the verification pass](../decisions/2026-10-04-verification-pass.md), [scrutiny levels](../decisions/2026-10-04-scrutiny-levels.md), [the review plan and routes](../decisions/2026-10-04-review-plan-and-routes.md), which supersedes the ban on committed routes, [credential files](../decisions/2026-10-04-credential-files.md), [the review ledger](../decisions/2026-10-04-review-ledger.md), [Actions completing the manifest](../decisions/2026-10-04-actions-complete-the-manifest.md), [Enola](../decisions/2026-10-04-enola.md), and [the research directory](../decisions/2026-10-04-research-directory.md).
