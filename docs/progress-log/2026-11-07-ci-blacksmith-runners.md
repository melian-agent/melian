The three Linux `check` jobs in `.github/workflows/check.yml` now run on Blacksmith's `blacksmith-4vcpu-ubuntu-2404` runners instead of `ubuntu-latest`, by the maintainer's ruling, to shorten the gate that every pull request and merge group waits on. On `ubuntu-latest` the jobs took 8 to 13 minutes. The job names, and so the required checks, are unchanged. The macOS job stays on `macos-latest`, and `melian-review-group.yml` and `claim.yml` stay on GitHub's runners, since each takes under a minute.
