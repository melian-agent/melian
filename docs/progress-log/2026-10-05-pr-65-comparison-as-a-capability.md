[Pull request #65](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/65) designs comparison with external reviewers as a Melian capability and adds it to the plan as milestone 2's step 15, not started. The hand-written comparison records had stopped yielding goldens or checks: the records for [pull requests #55](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/55), [#60](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/60), and [#61](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/61) owe none, and none says why Melian missed a finding. [design.md](../design.md#comparison-with-external-reviewers) now sets out `melian compare`. It normalises external findings to one shape and imports them from GitHub review threads by author login, CodeRabbit first, or from a file. It matches them to Melian's by site, and records the maintainer's adjudication with one miss reason from `owned-missed`, `no-owner`, `needs-execution`, and `out-of-scope`. `stats`, `backlog`, and `export` follow, and the markdown record becomes an export of the stored document. Steps 6 and 11 take `needs-execution` misses as scope, and milestone 4 gains a `Decider` question for matching. [The evals guideline](../guidelines/evals.md#comparisons) states the drain rule, which holds from this pull request on: every third record is followed by a backlog pull request that ships at least two owed goldens and re-measures their lens. [The decision](../decisions/2026-10-05-comparison-as-a-capability.md) supersedes the milestone 1 parallel-track rules that made every difference a golden.
