The walkthrough now runs beside the lenses and verification, for [issue #254](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/254). `reviewChangeset` calls a new `lensesStarted` hook once it has recorded its lens run, and `melian review` starts the summariser there, awaiting it only before output and before closing storage. `summarizeReview` takes the review's `origin` and `plan` from its caller, since adjudication has not yet written the provenance it used to read. Rounds on 2026-10-26 and 2026-10-27 spent 13 to 16 seconds on the walkthrough after everything else had finished; [the decision](../decisions/2026-11-08-walkthrough-beside-the-lenses.md) records why it starts at the lens run rather than at once.

A repeat review attaches to a live walkthrough task only on the light route it wants; it aborts one on another route and creates its own, which closes [issue #343](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/343).

A review harness now aborts every live walkthrough task as it opens, so a crashed review's walkthrough never resumes on the old route, and an aborted walkthrough costs no attempt.
