# 2026-10-25: high signal, low noise

The README promised that Melian is **Quiet**, and [pull request #201](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/201)'s review broke it with about 50 lines of caller-context notes repeated under eight lenses. [Issue #212](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/212) records the case. The maintainer named the principle **high signal, low noise**, for everything Melian shows a person. [The decision](../decisions/2026-10-25-high-signal-low-noise.md) states it, then its rules: every line earns its place, a fact is stated once, and diagnostics go to the ledger, `--json` or telemetry. It names what the rule never cuts, and it amends [the register decision](../decisions/2026-10-24-finding-register.md) so an element's defined place may be the ledger or the JSON; on conflict, this rule wins. The README's bullet takes the new name, `docs/design.md` gains the principle beside "Ask the authority", and [the findings guideline](../guidelines/findings.md) points at it.

The design lens could not have held a change to the rule: the hook in `packages/pipeline/src/lens-tools.ts` refuses a finding under any rule a lens does not declare, and none of its rules named a contradicted decision. The maintainer chose a generic built-in fallback, `contradicts-documented-decision`: a change that contradicts what the repository's decisions, design or stated principles say in words, where no more specific rule names the defect. The lens body says when to use it, that it yields to `fail-open-default`, `criterion-selection-bias` and the other rules, that its baseline is an active base text only, and that the finding quotes the documented words at the base. The `conventions` hand-off now says words in a decision or design section are `design`'s. The design lens's version moves from `ffc3d1482acf` to `768a63490995`, pinned in `packages/core/test/lens.test.ts`.

Four scripted goldens cover the rule: `design-contradicts-documented-decision` (a head that returns Sydney local time where a decision requires UTC ISO 8601), its clean twin, `design-contradicts-documented-decision-noise` (a head that prints a shared note once per lens against a base design principle) and its clean twin. `packages/evals/test/design-fixtures.test.ts` proves each fixture does what its golden claims. Before the rule, both positive goldens failed with `rule contradicts-documented-decision is not one of this lens's rules`, and the lens test failed on the rule list; both pass after it. No live run yet: the coordinator runs the design goldens live.
