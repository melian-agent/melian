For [pull request #296](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/296), fix pass 31 stops a review moved to another computer from reusing the first computer's probe results. It also merges `main`. The fix began with failing tests.

- A probing run's results are reused when its key matches, and the key named who is trusted, the time limit and the paths a probe may read, but not the platform or the sandbox the probes ran under (`e20215ab`). A review store moved from a Linux host to a macOS one would therefore reuse the Linux results. The probing environment now holds both, read from the sandbox the probes run under, and a probe refuses to open under a sandbox that differs from the one its environment names. The `melian round` at `28ae9b20` raised it as `c3dea23f7748153e`; the coordinator chose the fix, which [issue #288](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/288#issuecomment-6867933407) records.
  - A second review of one head under another platform, or under another kind of sandbox, reused the first before the fix and runs its probes again after. One under the same sandbox still reuses it.
  - A probe whose environment names another platform or sandbox ran before the fix; after it, the probe is refused and nothing runs.
  - The key changes, so every stored probing run is planned and probed once more on its next review, by design.
- `main` merged in at `43716d89`, with no conflict. [The identity guideline](../guidelines/identity.md)'s row for the lens-task attach key now names the two host facts and the reinstall gap (`73493506`).
