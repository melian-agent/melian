# Issue 561: the parity arm held to its total by what its lenses recorded, and the design lens's ceiling as loaded

The host round on [pull request #582](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/582)'s head f970e33e found that the parity arm could spend more than the committed arm's total. The coordinator directed one pass for four findings, each test first.

A lens's `budget.tokens` share is a soft limit: the request that crosses it finishes, and with `ended: count` its findings count. Two lenses at 50,000-token shares that each make one 80,000-token request spend 160,000 against a total of 100,000. `measureParity` now sums the budgeted tokens of every `melian.lens` stage the parity review recorded, planners and probers included. When the sum passes the total, the attempt is saved unscored, with the tokens spent and the overrun in its `said`, and its `overrun` field holds the excess. The evals guideline says a share is a soft limit and the sum is what keeps the promise, and the run-record template lists each overrun. On f970e33e the new test's second parity review spent 61 tokens against a total of 60 and was scored `unadjudicated`. Now it is left unscored with `overrun: 1`, and the first review, at 60, is still scored. Two existing tests whose parity stages had spent 90 against 60 now use stages within the total.

`parityToolCeiling = 400` called itself the design lens's committed ceiling, and `main` commits 600. `parityFindings = 16` made the same claim for the findings budget. Both constants are gone. `ParityArm.routed()` reads the findings budget and tool ceiling from the root design lens as loaded at the policy commit, at its deep level when it has one, and refuses when no design lens sits at the root. On f970e33e the new test's design lens of 20 findings and 600 tools still gave every lens 16 and 400.

`checkoutCommit` let git's stderr reach the terminal raw, and its error quoted the directory with its control bytes. Git's stderr is now captured, and the error passes through core's `visibleText`. On f970e33e the new test, with a missing directory named `gone\u001b[31m`, found the raw escape byte in the child's output.

The two injection flags on `graphGuidance` were refuted, and the constant is unchanged. Its text is the second and third sentences of the paragraph at line 8 of `.melian/lenses/design/LENS.md`, from "To test an assumption against the code that relies on it" to "before citing it", and it matches that text byte for byte on this head and on `main`. The paragraph's first sentence names design files and is not copied. Reading the sentences from the loaded lens would mean cutting its prompt at hand-chosen text, which drifts silently when the lens is reworded, so the arm keeps the copy.
