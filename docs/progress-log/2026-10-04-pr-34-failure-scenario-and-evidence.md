Every lens finding now carries a failure scenario and evidence locations, in [pull request #34](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/34), so the verifier has a claim to attack. `report_finding` requires a scenario of at most 2,000 characters and one to ten locations, each a `cause` or `context`, at head or at the base for deleted lines; Melian reads and stores the code at each. Only a `cause` location overlapping the change, new lines at head or old lines at the base, makes a finding outside the diff `affected`, which [the evidence locations decision](../decisions/2026-10-04-evidence-locations.md) records, superseding the rule that a base location never promotes. The terminal, JSON, and GitHub renderings, the lens instructions, and the goldens carry both fields, and the scripted runner checks them. Findings, verdicts, and pending rounds stored before read tolerantly: a single old evidence location becomes one `cause` at head, and a head published before the upgrade is not posted again.
