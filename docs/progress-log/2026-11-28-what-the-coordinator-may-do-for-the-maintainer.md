# What the coordinator may do for the maintainer

Coordinator.

AGENTS.md's Who does what and Ready and queued now say what the coordinator may do in the maintainer's place: dismiss an equivalent mutant, and dismiss a refuted injection flag on a prompt line the change only moved. Every policy notice, the override status and a finding that restates a ruling are the maintainer's, and the permissions he had given for those in the coordinator's session are withdrawn. [The decision](../decisions/2026-11-28-what-the-coordinator-may-do-for-the-maintainer.md) says why.

The first reviewed head, 268c96a02, wrote down five delegations. Codex's review, the review of record and two Melian rounds found each of three resting on an agent's judgement and contradicting an active decision; the fixes in 5e8689332, bd0d35dfa, c8dd4d3d8 and b9e2ebd41 narrowed them and the second round found new holes of the same class. The maintainer then chose to keep two and withdraw three.

No code changes, so nothing here guards trust or durability and no mutant owes a claim.
