Melian's fifteenth round on [pull request #89](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/89) left five findings above advisory besides the policy notice. Four held. The fourteenth round's guard counted every raw SARIF result, so a report whose only results were suppressed or resolved failed the check, against the empty-exit-one decision. The guard also ran only when nothing survived, so a dropped `node_modules` result beside a kept one vanished. `#sarif` now counts the results Enola reported that are neither resolved nor suppressed, and fails closed with `invalidOutput` at any exit code when fewer survive normalisation than were counted. A suppressed-only exit 1 stays clean with its note. Two tests were added for gaps: `ToolInventory.open` now has a test that its cache root follows `MELIAN_STATE_DIR`, and the coverage cache's identity guards on store and read are reached by artifacts for another tree and version, where the old assertion failed earlier on a missing graph.

The fifth finding, that a caller section differing across a crash and replay replaces the lens task, did not hold as a defect. The pipeline guideline states it as the design, and `review.test.ts` already proves that changed callers replace the run and identical callers attach. Whether a transient `enola impact` failure should be allowed to cost a rerun of every lens is a design question for the owner, not a bug fixed here.
