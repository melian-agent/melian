`melian review` in [pull request #19](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/19) runs the deterministic checks from [pull request #18](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/18) before the lenses and passes their records to the review, since `reviewChangeset` does not call `runChecks`; without them every default-tier review read not reviewed. `--rerun` reruns failed checks as well as failed lenses, and `melian doctor` reports whether Biome and tsc come from the checkout or Melian's own copy. Having `reviewChangeset` drive the checks itself remains a follow-up.
