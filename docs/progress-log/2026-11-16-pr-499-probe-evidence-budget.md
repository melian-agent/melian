# The verifier is shown at most 128 KiB of a candidate's probe evidence

[Pull request #499](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/499), for [issue #357](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/357), bounds the probe evidence the verifier is shown for one candidate. `boundProbeEvidence` in `packages/pipeline/src/probing.ts` renders evidence that fits byte for byte as before. Past 128 KiB it cuts the plan, the script, each file and the outputs to the largest size every part may keep and still fit, keeping each part's ends, and a note outside the boundary says what was cut. The stored probes and reruns stay whole, and a rerun reads them whole. [The decision](../decisions/2026-11-16-probe-evidence-bounded.md) records the choice.

Codex's first adversarial review found that a candidate whose least cut still passed the budget was rendered over it. The coordinator chose to fail it closed. Commit 7d038c00 asks no verifier of such a candidate, and the review reads not reviewed, with a reason naming the budget and the count of probing claims. Its tests failed first at the head before it. The unit test's count of merged claims never ended, since every count rendered (`expected 100 to be +0`), and the review test read `expected 'findings' to be 'not-reviewed'`. Both pass after the fix. Codex's second review found that a fixed budget does not follow the route's model. The coordinator chose to record the limit, and commit ff22ab81 names it in the decision with the arithmetic and says a route-aware budget is not built.

Melian's first round, at 5c9448dc, blocked twice. The design lens found that the verification request's key held a cut rendering that only cut parts to nothing, so a change to how much a partial cut keeps moved no key. Commit 812fd2f6 adds a claim cut partway by the prompt's own search to that rendering. Its new test in `test/probe-evidence-renderings.test.ts` failed at 5c9448dc, since the rendering held no partial cut, and passes after the fix. tsc rejected a verifier reply's type in `test/probing-level.test.ts`, and commit 6e69f4d5 fixed it.

The [mutant inventory](2026-11-16-pr-499-mutants.json) lists the 212 mutants of `probing.ts` and `verification.ts` at 812fd2f6, whose source c83d761a keeps; 48 are ignored by configuration. Each of the other 164 was run by hand at c83d761a, against its named test alone after that test passed unmutated. 152 failed their test. Five more, in the search's loop, made it run forever, and each named test hung alone until the runner stopped it by its recorded pids. The other 7 are equivalent, each with its reason. Commit c83d761a adds the test at the exact bound of the fail-closed check, which kills `>=` for `>` there.

Melian's second round, at dd2fa4a3, raised the same class again: the key held a cut over fixed samples, so a change the samples did not exercise left a real prompt's cut outside the key. That is the identity guideline's missing input, its rule against keying a sample rendering. The coordinator chose to key each cut candidate's real evidence, from the `boundProbeEvidence` call the spawn phase makes, and to delete the sample rendering, its golden and its tests. The new tests in `test/verification-request-cut.test.ts` hold a request with no probe evidence, and one whose evidence fits, to the keys main computed at 2fae681a. They show that a change to the cut changes the keys of cut candidates' requests alone, and that a refused candidate keys stably. All four failed at dd2fa4a3 and pass after the fix. This closes the class, whose two instances were the design findings of rounds one and two.

Codex's third adversarial review, at 33727a11, found that the key cut its evidence under the key's fixed nonce, 24 zeroes, while the prompt cut it under the review's. Quoting replaces the nonce wherever the head's text holds it, so a probe script of zeroes rendered far shorter for the key: the key saw no cut where the verifier was shown one. The key now cuts under the review's nonce and then names the boundary by the fixed one. The new test in `test/verification-request-cut.test.ts`, a script of zeroes just under the budget, failed at 33727a11 with no cut in the key (`.toMatch() expects to receive a string, but got undefined`), and passes after the fix, keying the same under two review nonces.

A [second mutant inventory](2026-11-16-pr-499-mutants-key.json) lists the 35 mutants in `verification.ts` over `dd2fa4a3...c0e9ed28`, the key's two fix commits. Six are string literals, ignored by configuration. Each of the other 29 was run by hand at c0e9ed28, against its named test alone after that test passed unmutated, and all 29 failed it.

Codex's fourth adversarial review, at 9bb29c1a, found two cases that fail closed. A candidate of many tiny parts can be refused when a larger keep would fit, and a verification task stored past its spawn phase before this change keeps its whole prompt. The coordinator chose to record both, and the decision now names each limit and its remedy.

The review of record, at 1b7fa623, found that the plan was cut as one part. A cut kept only the plan's two ends, so the row of the attack a probe answered, which says what a failing probe of it shows, usually fell in the cut. A verifier shown that cut could confirm nothing. The coordinator chose to keep that row whole and cut the attacks before and after it as two parts. The new test in `test/probe-evidence-bound.test.ts`, one to four claims at their limits answering attack 12, failed at 1b7fa623 (`expected '## Claim c1's probe…' to contain '12. c1 target 11 begins…'`) and passes after the fix. The refusal test's last count of merged claims that fits moves from 19 to 18, and it still checks that count and one past it. Evidence that fits renders as before, and `test/verification-request-cut.test.ts` still holds a fitting request to main's key. It also found two doc comments in `probing.ts` that named a missing test file and the deleted sample key; commit 594457ab corrects them.

A [third mutant inventory](2026-11-16-pr-499-mutants-row.json) lists the mutants of `1b7fa623...7da2934f`, which take in main's merge, with claims for `probing.ts` alone: 17 there, 6 ignored by configuration. Melian's fourth round, at 335a31ca, raised one acknowledge finding from the design lens. A candidate whose evidence no cut fits is asked nothing, but `startVerification` still unlocked its route's providers, so a credential command could run for a route no request uses, which [the credentials decision](../decisions/2026-10-08-credentials-follow-concrete-task-routes.md) forbids. The coordinator chose to fix it. The unlock now leaves out each candidate whose evidence `boundProbeEvidence` refuses. The new test in `test/probing-level.test.ts`, a refused candidate whose verifier runs on a provider of its own, failed at 335a31ca (`expected true to be false`, the provider unlocked) and passes after the fix; its pair shows that a candidate whose evidence fits still unlocks its verifier's provider. The maintainer's ruling on [pull request #477](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/477), that a commit refused for want of an unlock then unlocks every provider, is untouched: that path unlocks the same list, which now holds every provider a request can use.
