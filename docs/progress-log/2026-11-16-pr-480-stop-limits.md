On [issue #480](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/480)'s branch, [pull request #498](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/498), the decision now names what a stop can still get wrong, by [the maintainer's ruling of 2026-11-16](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/498#issuecomment-6910822936), given in the coordinator's session. No code changed.

- Codex's review of `6768d625` found that the budget is checked only when a read starts, so the synchronous matching of records against a read could hold a very large tree's stop past it.
- The same review found that a stop counts a process ended once SIGKILL is sent, so a process in an uninterruptible wait survives with no `childrenLeft`. [Issue #507](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/507) is to read the table after SIGKILL, here and in the mutation supervisor.
- The Melian round at `6768d625`, in its design lens, found that a stopped call can settle about fifteen seconds past a deadline the record store's decision called one deadline for the whole call. The decision now amends that decision's Time row: a deadline bounds when a call's last git command starts. A state-git write whose push a slow stop ends near its deadline says the push may have landed, even when it did.
