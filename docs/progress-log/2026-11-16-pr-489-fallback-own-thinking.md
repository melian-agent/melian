A route's fallback may now name its own thinking level, `{ model, thinking }`, for [issue #489](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/489): a lens, prober, verifier, triage and walkthrough ask each fallback at its own level, and at the route's level when it names none. `melian doctor` prints each fallback's level and warns when a bare fallback inherits `off`, `minimal` or `low` from a first model of another provider, the case where Claude at `low` failed over from Codex and called no tool. Melian's own light and medium Claude fallbacks run at `medium`, heavy's Opus at `medium`, and the design lens's Opus stays at `high`. [The decision](../decisions/2026-11-16-fallback-own-thinking.md) records why, and [the design](../design.md#the-review-plan) and [the identity guideline](../guidelines/identity.md) say what each key now holds.
