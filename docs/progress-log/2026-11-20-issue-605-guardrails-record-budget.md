# Issue #605: a guardrails record fits the store

[Issue #605](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/605): a head that changes many workspace manifests wrote a guardrails check record larger than the git store takes, so the store refused the round's records.

- Core defines the record limit once, `recordLimit`, 4 MiB. The git store's tree reader, the relay's per-file bound and every store's `add` read it. The relay's batch bound is twice it, 8 MiB, by the maintainer's ruling of 2026-11-20.
- Every store, memory, directory and git, refuses a record past the limit with `invalidRecord` through `SharedRecord.storable()`, measuring the whole batch before it writes. The conformance suite tests a record at the limit and one a byte past it.
- `SightingBudget.checkRecord` gives a check record's sightings the limit less a 4 KiB envelope allowance. The guardrails check fits its joined findings to it: in ID order, a finding keeps its detail while the full findings so far and every later one abridged fit, and each later one is `Finding.abridged`, with a fixed note naming how many files lost their detail. IDs, severities and resolutions are unchanged, so the blocking set is too.
- An abridged finding keeps the shorter of its What and its headline, in UTF-8 bytes, and the trim leaves whole a finding that abridging would not make smaller. Before, a headline longer than What grew the finding, so trimming could lift a record further past the limit. The note now says that `git diff` shows the files whose detail it left out.
- A record that cannot fit even with every finding abridged fails the guardrails check with `RecordError` `recordTooLarge`, by the maintainer's ruling on the issue, so the review reads not reviewed and its records export. Before, the export threw `invalidRecord` with a verdict of findings already stored. [The decision](../decisions/2026-11-20-guardrails-record-fits-the-store.md) records the ruling and the builder's choice, agreed by the coordinator, to trim the check's own findings.

Tests: `packages/core/test/sighting-budget.test.ts` for the bound, a byte past it, the cut, determinism, the floor and a byte under it, and no finding growing; `packages/core/test/record-limit-utf8.test.ts` for multi-byte records at the limit; `packages/pipeline/test/guardrails-record-limit.test.ts`, which reviews 2,200 changed manifests whose record cannot fit and reads not reviewed; the new conformance case in `packages/core/src/testing.ts`; the relay bounds in `packages/pipeline/test/record-relay.test.ts`; and `packages/cli/test/guardrails-record-budget.test.ts`, which runs the guardrails check over 1,500 changed workspace manifests and adds the trimmed record to a git store whole.

Mutation proof for the no-growth and fail-closed commits, which guard durability: [the inventory](2026-11-20-issue-605-trim-mutants.json) enumerates `e17e59ca5...b32f74378` with claims in `packages/core/src/sighting-budget.ts` and `packages/core/src/findings.ts`. At `b32f74378`, a scratch runner applied each of the 45 generated mutants, checked the text it replaced, and ran the named test alone: every named test passed alone unmutated (18 of 18), and all 45 failed mutated. 43 are killed by the tests written failing-first; 2, the longest-note bound in `fit`, are killed by the bound test added for them. None is equivalent and none survives. The 10 ignored rows are string literals.

Fix pass, from Codex's adversarial review: only the guardrails check applied the budget, so enough valid Biome, tsc or Enola findings, up to 16 MiB of tool output, completed the check and adjudication and then failed the export with a verdict of findings standing. `runCheck` in `packages/pipeline/src/checks.ts` now fits every check's findings, and the fixed sentence of `recordTooLarge` names no check. `packages/pipeline/test/static-record-limit.test.ts` reviews 12,000 Biome findings whose record cannot fit and reads not reviewed, with its account, failed check and round exported.
