The `melian round` workflow now runs probes, by the maintainer's ruling of 2026-11-16, in the coordinator's session, recorded [on issue #505](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/505#issuecomment-6910823418). [Pull request #515](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/515) builds [the revised plan](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/505#issuecomment-6911137332). [The decision](../decisions/2026-11-16-round-job-probes-behind-containment.md) gives the reasons, and the coordinator's answers on approach that it records are not rulings.

The round job installs bubblewrap, socat and ripgrep, and lifts the runner's AppArmor limit on unprivileged user namespaces. Before any step holds a login, `scripts/round-probe-containment.mjs` runs one probe through the real `Probe` class. It plants a canary under the round's login names, in the home and temporary directories and in a System V segment, then tries every route out: the environment, the command line, each readable file, writes, TCP, DNS, both proxies, `sudo` and IPC. It fails the round unless each is shut. `check.yml` runs the same script, so a change that opens a route fails CI first. The first runs found System V IPC open, so `packages/pipeline/src/probe.ts` now starts bubblewrap under `unshare --user --ipc`.

The round passes `--require-probes`. Under it a level set to probe that cannot probe here, or whose prober's probe could not run, records `failed` with one cause from a closed set of eight. `--probe-gaps <file>` writes each gap. `scripts/round-probe-gaps.mjs` prints one line per gap to the job's log, and the round's comment says only that the check could not probe and that the log says why, since a level's own reason can name a path in the head. The stored field for the cause is deferred, as the decision says.

Tests. The containment script's decisions are functions with their own tests: `containmentPlan`, `judge`, `unfinished`, `observeHost` and `outcome`. Each failing input has a test, and an empty failure list is the one that passes, by the coordinator's answer on approach of 2026-11-16, [recorded on the issue](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/505#issuecomment-6917237848). `main` is I/O alone, and tests through a fake `Probe` cover its unavailable sandbox, its unfinished probe, its cleanup and its entry guard. The step itself runs as a Linux test that fails rather than skips under `MELIAN_PROBE_SANDBOX=1`. `packages/pipeline/test/probing-level.test.ts` covers each cause and each level that still passes. `packages/pipeline/test/probe-ipc.test.ts` shows a probe cannot see the host's segment. The workflow tests in `scripts/melian-round-workflow.test.mjs` hold the install, the containment step's place before the logins, and the flags.
