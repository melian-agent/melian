Melian's own rounds now probe in a second repository lens, by the maintainer's rulings of 2026-11-16, in the coordinator's session. [Pull request #494](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/494) first gave the `trust-boundary` lens's own `deep` level `probe`. Its review of record found that a level that probes reports only findings a failing probe backs, so that lens would stop reporting what it finds by reading. The maintainer ruled that probing is not complete until one review both reads and probes.

So `.melian/lenses/trust-boundary/LENS.md` is back as `main` holds it, and reads at every level. `.melian/lenses/trust-boundary-probes/LENS.md` extends it, adds one body line, and gives `deep` `probe: { prober: medium }` with the same budgets. The root `melian.yaml` adds `lens.trust-boundary-probes` to the `full` tier, with `trust-boundary`'s paths and `level: { floor: deep }`. No tier or route changed. [The decision](../decisions/2026-11-16-probing-on-for-melians-own-rounds.md) lists the rulings and the costs, and names [issue #497](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/497), a level that probes and also reads, as what retires the second lens.
