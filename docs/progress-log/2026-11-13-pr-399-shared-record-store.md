Step 46's build 1, for [issue #392](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/392), landed in [pull request #399](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/399). It gives core the shared record of [the shared record decision](../decisions/2026-11-13-shared-record-behind-a-store-port.md). `SharedRecord` validates an envelope by rules 2 to 4 and names it by the SHA-256 of its RFC 8785 bytes. `RecordSet` computes from the set alone what `supersedes` does and which rounds stand. `RecordStore` is the port, with `MemoryRecordStore` and `DirectoryRecordStore` as its adapters. The conformance suite in `@melian-agent/core/testing` has one case per promise of rule 3, and `test/record-store.test.ts` runs each case against an adapter built to break that promise. Two shapes the decision leaves to prose are this build's: a round carries its revision and `payload.replaces`, a sorted list of record IDs, and a dismissal's envelope carries no revision. Codex's adversarial review found that a dying `add` could leave a record listed after disposal, and that any caller could write the disposal mark. Round 1 found a leading byte order mark dropped on read. The host round from the branch's own build found three more defects: writes followed a symlinked directory out of the store, `list` answered empty when the store vanished mid-walk, and a record's payload could be mutated under its ID. The pull request's report comments name the fix commit and the failing-test run for each. The mutation inventory is `2026-11-13-pr-399-mutants.json`: 324 mutants ignored by configuration, 837 killed and 11 equivalent. Its 30 conformance-suite mutants stay unclaimed. The maintainer ruled on 2026-11-14, in the coordinator's session, that they are listed with a reason under the label "suite assertion, proved per case", and `2026-11-13-pr-399-suite-mutants.json` lists them.
