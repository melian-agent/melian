Lenses declare scrutiny levels and their token and tool budgets are enforced, in [pull request #37](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/37), the levels half of milestone 2 step 3. `LENS.md` maps `quick`, `careful`, and `deep` to a tier, a reading scope, whether the level's findings are verified, and a budget, each field inheriting from the top level; a lens that declares none runs only at `careful`, and a bad level is an error naming the lens and the level. The built-in lenses declare all three: `quick` on `medium` with a small budget, `careful` as before, and `deep` on `heavy` reading the functions around each hunk. Every lens runs at `careful` until triage lands in step 5. A lens conversation now ends after the round that spends its tokens, counted from Pi's usage record, or its calls to the read-only tools, counted by call ID so a replay counts once, and keeps the findings it reported; [the lens budgets decision](../decisions/2026-10-04-lens-budgets.md) records what each counts and why such a lens records `ran`. Each lens's check record names its level and any budget that ended it, and the verdict keeps the checks that ran, so the terminal and JSON output show both. The five new lenses follow in the next pull request.
