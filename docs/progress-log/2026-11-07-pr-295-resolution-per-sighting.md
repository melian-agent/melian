Step 21, "Resolution per sighting", [pull request #295](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/295) for [issue #230](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/230). Two lenses that sight one finding ID now each resolve as a report of their own: under their own check, the severity they reported after any lowering, their own mapping and ceiling, and their own unjudged cap, with a refuted sighting left out while another stands. Before, the findings document's merge at the highest severity made one lens's ceiling or mapping apply to another lens's severity, and one unjudged sighting capped a confirmed one. The cause caps still read the merged cause, so a merge never lowers what blocks. `Finding.capped` reads each sighting from the claims the findings document already keeps with their reported severity, so no stored shape changes; a finding without a claim of its ID for every producer it lists keeps the merged reading. [The decision](../decisions/2026-11-07-resolution-per-sighting.md) amends the stated limit in [the acknowledge-status decision](../decisions/2026-10-17-acknowledge-status-exact-rule.md), which superseded the as-built resolution decision and restated it. `packages/pipeline/test/resolution-per-sighting.test.ts` reproduces both defects through the findings document, and `packages/core/test/finding-resolution.test.ts` replaces the test that pinned the limit.
