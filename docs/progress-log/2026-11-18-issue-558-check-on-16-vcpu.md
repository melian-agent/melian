The check's three required Linux jobs moved from 4 to 16 vCPU Blacksmith runners, for [issue #558](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/558). A throwaway workflow on [pull request #560](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/560) first measured 13 arms, six runs each, in [the run record](../research/2026-11-18-ci-runner-sizes.md). A Linux job took 11.7 minutes at the median on 4 vCPU and 3.9 on 16, with no test failing at any size. A larger worker count or a shard gained nothing more. [The decision](../decisions/2026-11-18-check-runs-on-16-vcpu.md) records the choice and its estimated cost of about $0.10 more a run, about $10 a week or $0.90 a merge at the week's 100 runs for 11 merges. The ten runs after the change are to be recorded beside the 18.2 minutes before it.
