# Step 26: the audit of partial `Supersedes:` lines

[Issue #238](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/238) closes step 26. The attack on [pull request #150](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/150) counted 36 `Supersedes:` lines that retired a whole decision while changing part of it, but recorded no list. This audit read all 83 edges that resolve on main at `27797250`, 80 of which existed at the attack. Each pair was read whole, with the active decisions its chain ends in, and each lost clause was checked against code and docs. 24 edges are whole retirements. 59 are narrow, all from before the attack: the successor changed part of its target and restated none of the rest, so 385 distinct clauses lost their standing. More edges qualify than the attack's 36 because a successor that says the rest "stands" or "remains" without restating it counts as narrow. The [phase 1 census](2026-10-18-step26-phase1-supersession-rule.md#hand-census) judged 56 of its rows useful by the same test.

A superseded file stays inactive whatever later happens to its successor, and merged decision files are never edited. So each narrow edge is repaired by a new decision that amends the active heads its chain ends in and restates the lost clauses that still hold. 17 amendments, one per family of related targets, restate 286 clauses. 99 were dropped: obsolete on main, already stated by another active decision, in conflict with one, or unsettled. An amendment wins where it conflicts with its target, so a doubtful clause was dropped rather than restated. The mutation decision needs no amendment: [the reruns decision](../decisions/2026-10-16-mutation-reruns-later-changes.md) restates every clause of it that holds. No existing decision file changed, and the head graph resolves with a 45 KB index against its 64 KiB bound.

The table below gives every edge's verdict and reason. [A comment on issue #238](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/238#issuecomment-6718216607) lists every dropped clause with why it went, the conflicting and unsettled ones first for the maintainer's ruling. The maintainer ruled on 2026-11-08, in the builder's session, on the eleven drops marked conflicting or unsettled. The publishing-credentials clause is restated in the ledger amendment. The ledger's section order goes to a follow-up that records the order the renderer uses. The other nine stay dropped. Two gaps surfaced outside this step. `docs/design/interaction-model.md` lists the ledger's sections in an order the renderer no longer follows. The comment on the check workflow's `origin/main` fetch names a triage gate that no longer needs it.
