A review now writes its shared records as its tasks finish, and a round can be decided from those records alone, for [issue #425](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/425) in [pull request #476](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/476). Core gains a payload check for each record kind, and an account record, the sixth kind, by the maintainer's ruling of 2026-11-16 that [the account decision](../decisions/2026-11-16-account-record.md) records: a round names one account, which lists the manifest, the producers, the skips allowed and the checks no record carries. `ReviewRecords` in the pipeline builds each record, and `reviewChangeset` exports each lens run as its lens ends and the rest once adjudication decides. `RecordedRound` decides a round from its records, and `test/recorded-round.test.ts` holds its verdict equal to the review's when the round passes, blocks, and is not reviewed. `melian review --records dir:<path>` exports to a directory store, and `melian findings --records dir:<path>` reads each round back; both count records only when the base's `melian.yaml` trusts writers. A check record now has its own producer, `check.record`, apart from the check run's request, and the identity guideline gains rows for it and for the round. Host verdicts order their checks by `inManifestOrder`, so a reader rebuilds the same verdict; that changes a verdict's fingerprint once, as [the pipeline guideline](../guidelines/pipeline.md#shared-records) says. Dismissal records are [issue #481](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/issues/481), and until they land a dismissed finding blocks in the reader.
