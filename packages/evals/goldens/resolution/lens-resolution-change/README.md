# A change to a LENS.md declared resolution draws a notice, and a guardrails ceiling of advisory lowers neither

A change to a `LENS.md` declared resolution draws a notice too, and a `guardrails` ceiling of `advisory` lowers neither. The root switches the review off, sets its notices to P1, and caps every guardrail finding at `advisory`. The head raises the cleanup lens's ceiling and lowers P1 in `services/melian.yaml`. Both notices are reported. No entry under `guardrails` applies to either, so each resolves `block` through the built-in table.
