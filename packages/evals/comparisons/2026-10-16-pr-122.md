## Patterns

- **The design lens read each new decision against the active ones, and found a contradiction every round until the decisions shrank.** `65438fe4` (compare against comparison-sites), `3331116b` and `c5b12cf7` (mutation reuse against the exact-head mutation decision), `99d32952` (delta identity against review identity), `c0ef8384` (the selector against review identity) and `b9e062d7` (the supersession check against the index) are all one shape: a decision for unbuilt work changes a rule that another active decision states, and neither says so. Each fix tightened the text. The pass that ended the run held the designs as problem, choice and open questions, and round seven raised nothing above the notice.
- **Codex's five findings landed before round one, and Melian found the two with the most lines of text.** A1 and A2 are about contradictions between decisions, which `design` reads. A3 and A4 are claims about code and stored data that the decisions make and cannot check against, and a docs-only review has no check that reads the code a decision describes.
- **Two blocks in round four, both about stored identity.** A delta round that shares the revision key overwrites a full round, and a shared mutation partition is a new kind of stored object with no guard. They came at the same time as the maintainer's answers entered the readiness list. Both were fixed by a design change, not a wording change.

The rounds with a recorded time took 187 seconds (round one), 352 (round four), 232 (round five) and 233 (round six). Round two took 56 seconds and did not review.
