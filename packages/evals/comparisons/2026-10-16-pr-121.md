## Patterns

- **Folding instances into one finding failed three times, and the third review ended it.** A (identity, dismissal, cause), `ca6dc2d2` (round two, the trigger) and `88a56baf` (round three, the same trigger again) each found a new hole in the same design, and the author had fixed the earlier one in between. The class was escalated, not fixed once more. The revert to one finding per instance left no identity to get wrong. This is the rule the Converging section of `AGENTS.md` sets, applied inside the pull request that wrote the prompts.
- **A paragraph written for every lens contradicted a lens that works the other way.** `bf094789` (`contracts` reports at dependants the change left alone) is a shared instruction that fits most lenses and breaks `contracts`. Melian found it in round two, and no external reviewer did.
- **The golden for the new rule carried a second defect that a lens would also report.** A3 is the fixture's, not the prompt's: a golden whose production code is wrong beyond its declared defect scores the second defect as noise. The author made the golden declare both.

Neither external reviewer found `bf094789`. Melian found A1 and A2 on the revised wording, and missed only A3.
