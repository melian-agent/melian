## Maintainer decisions

The Opus review would overturn four of the pull request's calls: the escalated run standing alone, folder variants sharing a band, triage blind to routes, and a `--rerun` that retried only a recorded failure. The maintainer overturned all four.

- **A quick run's severe findings survive escalation until refuted.** Only an explicit refutation removes one; the verifier judges the rest. The decision file supersedes the scrutiny-levels decision's sentence on the escalated record. From A1 and C2.
- **Triage offers only levels a lens can run, and fails closed.** A band with no such level fails the review rather than run below the floor. From A2, C1, and B5.
- **A pull request's lenses stay at `careful` or above until triage is calibrated.** The head writes the change triage reads. From C4.
- **Bands are per variant, and the question is shared.** From C3.
- **A lens task is reused only under the same band and `escalateAt`; `--rerun` asks again after any incomplete decision.** From B10, C7, and B9.
- **Recorded answers name their version, and a nested file may not set `triage`.** From A3 and C11.

### For the maintainer: the plan's step 5 entry

At a9309dc the plan's step 5 entry still describes "an escalated run in its own conversation inside the lens task, whose record and findings stand for the lens". Since 5d47cdb the quick run's unrefuted findings at or above `escalateAt` count too. The decision file and `design.md` said so; the plan's line and the progress-log entry of [pull request #62](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/62) did not. Melian's second round did not raise it. The author corrected both in 8820a37.
