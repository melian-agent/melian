## Differences

Codex found six, all at the trust boundary or in the identity and lifecycle contracts. Opus found seventeen and reproduced thirteen of them with a failing test; four came from reading. Five of Opus's restate Codex's, and two more extend Codex's cause finding. The rest are Opus's alone, including the GitHub fingerprint requirement, the rewrap failure, and the dependence of identity on the model's wording, which neither Codex nor the earlier automated pass saw. Between them the reviews changed three decisions in [design.md](../../../docs/design.md): finding identity, the cause rule, and what a lens may report.

Each valid finding is a golden to be written for the evals corpus once step 5 of the [implementation plan](../../../docs/design-implementation-plan.md) defines the format.
