## Differences

Codex found four, each a way the new evidence could prove less, or store more, than it claims: a `cause` location nothing checks, a rename no location could name, a snippet with no byte bound, and a published record that forgets its revision. Only the byte bound was found by another reviewer. The Opus review checked A4 and judged it could not happen; the test proved Codex right, and the fix showed the defect predates this pull request. A1 is the one finding not fixed here: the design already answers it with the verifier's advisory cap, so it waits for step 6 behind a test that pins it.

The Opus review of record found nine, seven of its own. Its findings ask whether a claim survives the pipeline whole: through a merge, where a static member's empty claim replaced a lens's, and back to the lens, which could not see what its evidence resolved to. The rest are tests, design fidelity, and two upgrade limits. Its first two findings drove the maintainer decision on merged finding claims.

In the first round Melian found one, and it was a real defect in the code this pull request added: the GitHub comment said "deleted by this change" for lines the change never touched. This was Melian's first review of a milestone 2 pull request through the CLI. The Opus review raised the same defect, and Codex did not. Against the twelve distinct findings of the first round, Melian's recall is one in twelve and its precision one in one. It ran only its two built-in lenses, `correctness` and `contracts`; the `trust-boundary` and `durability` lenses that might find A1, A3, and A4 arrive in step 3.

The second round is the first time Melian's own review caught a defect that a review fix introduced. The Opus review's C3 asked for the byte cap and A3's fix implemented it; the fixer saw the ID change as a rare side effect, and Melian showed that it breaks a stated contract and reopens dismissals after the upgrade. No other reviewer ran on that head, so D1 to D3 overlap nothing. The live goldens run on 7e12c4f will be recorded separately under `packages/evals/runs/`.

Two findings overlap: A3 and C3, and B1 and C4. No reviewer raised a finding the maintainer judged noise.
