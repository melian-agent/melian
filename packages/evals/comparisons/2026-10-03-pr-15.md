## Differences

Codex found six, all at the trust boundary or in the lifecycle of findings and models: prompt injection, the findings race, budget replay, prose evidence, token expiry, and fallbacks. Opus found nineteen: sixteen reproduced with a failing test, and three by reading. Four of Opus's restate Codex's: B1 is A1, B3 is A2, B5 is A5, and B12 is A3. The rest are Opus's alone, including the double run after a crash, the stale findings of a disabled or retiered lens, the search attribute hole, and the stored `block` on pre-existing findings, none of which Codex or the earlier automated pass saw. Between them the reviews changed five decisions in [design.md](../../../docs/design.md): prompt boundaries, finding sightings, evidence for `affected`, review attachment, and resolution ownership.

## Live run

The first live run of the corpus, on Opus 5.5, is in [runs/2026-10-03-live-goldens.md](../runs/2026-10-03-live-goldens.md). Recall was 1.00 and the clean golden drew nothing, but precision was 0.50: on each golden with a defect, the lens that does not own it reported it again. Three changes followed, each its own commit. C1 renders every lens's rule IDs, severities, and budget into its instructions, since three of the four reporting lenses had opened with a rule the hook refused. C2 hands contract breaks and the callers they break to the contracts lens, and tells correctness to leave a defect none of its rules fits. C3 limits contracts to changed declared contracts and leaves a bug inside a function's body to correctness. The goldens' expectations are unchanged. The rerun's numbers will be in the next run file.
