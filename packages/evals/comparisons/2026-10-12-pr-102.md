## Parser designs

The branch changed parser design after each confirmed defect. The maintainer chose each replacement.

| Design | Why it was replaced | Replacement and fix |
|---|---|---|
| CommonMark line-pattern parser | It treated code, fenced examples, link text and titles as declarations, and disagreed with CommonMark fences and CRLF. | mdast became the syntax parser in `6d8dfa0b`. |
| mdast node positions | Positions preserved syntax but lost physical boundaries inside opaque multiline nodes. | Position-aware opaque spans in `978b87c9`; `673de959` also made malformed local destinations refuse parsing. |
| Source slices with a fail-closed guard | Source slices preserved physical lines but interpreted decoded entity newlines incorrectly and retained container prefixes. | `9075f432` read source-line prose and refused decoded/source disagreement. |
| Decoded text, declarations only in root paragraphs | This is the final grammar. It avoids container declarations while preserving decoded CommonMark text and the disagreement refusal. | `26ef3e9c`. The final round and Codex review of record found no substantive defect. |
