## Differences

Codex found five, all about the verdict's integrity: what it counts, what it may skip, which task may write it, what a merge may lower, and which task a repeat review trusts. Opus found twelve, eleven reproduced with a failing test and one by reading. Five of Opus's restate Codex's: B2 is A4, B4 is A1, B5 is A2, B7 is A3, and B9 is A5. The rest are Opus's alone, including the retarget hole, the dismissed-keeper hole, and the permanence of a failed task, none of which Codex found. Between them the reviews changed three decisions in [design.md](../../../docs/design.md): the check manifest, dedupe and lifecycle, and review identity.
