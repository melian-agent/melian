## Maintainer decisions

Eleven decisions came from the review and the live run, nine from the first round and two from round four. `design.md`, the lens backlog boundaries decision, the evals guideline, and the decision on a lens with no paths record them.

- **A hand-off is rendered only when its owner runs.** `correctness` lists the defects it leaves to a neighbour only in a review that runs that neighbour, so in the `standard` tier it keeps its whole coverage, contract changes included. From A1 and C2; abb0203 extended it to `contracts`.
- **Hostile control is proved by data flow or the base, never by the head's prose.** `trust-boundary` reports judge control or a fail-open check only when callers, entry points, or the base show an author's input reaching a result trusted outside the run, and leaves the revision's own tests, plugins, and build configuration alone. `removed-behaviour` excuses a replacement only for a purpose the base or the design states. From A2 and C3.
- **The injection rule's severity is always allowed.** `report_finding` accepts `melian/injection-attempt` at P1 from every lens, whatever severities it declares. From B11.
- **Fixture standards are inert in the repository.** Goldens store `AGENTS.golden.md` and `melian.golden.yaml`, live only in the golden's own repository, and Melian's `melian.yaml` keeps every lens off the goldens. From A5 and C12.
- **A lens whose paths match no changed file is an allowed skip.** A change of goldens alone selected no lens, and read not reviewed. The lens now records `skipped` with the reason `no paths`, and the change passes on its deterministic checks. A follow-up to A5's fix, in bdcd418.
- **An example never restates a golden.** From C1.
- **The bars are written down.** Over three passes, a lens's goldens need a worst precision of at least 0.8 and a worst recall of at least 0.6. A lens below either ships marked so, and its goldens are never loosened. From A4 and C4.
- **A tuning comparison is controlled.** Both lens versions run three times over the same corpus and code. From A4 and C4.
- **A clean golden that draws a right finding is corrected, not the lens.** `removed-behaviour` reported that `trust-boundary-clean-build-config`'s move from `tsc` to esbuild dropped the only type check. It was right, so 9243d0a keeps `tsc --noEmit` in both trees. From the live run.
- **A moved file is covered by its old path's lens.** A lens selected through a rename's old path may report in the moved file. From E5.
- **A rule stays broad, and the hand-off narrows it.** `unhandled-error` keeps deleted rethrows, so `correctness` alone still reports one, and only the conditional hand-off leaves them to `removed-behaviour`. From E3.
