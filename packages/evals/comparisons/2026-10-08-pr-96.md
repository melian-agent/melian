Pull request: [melian-agent/melian#96](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/96), step 14 "What step 3 left", on branch `step-14`. It carries six items. Item 1 puts the enclosing function of each hunk in the change prompt. Item 2 leaves a review not reviewed when a lens hands work to a neighbour that has reached its findings cap. Item 3 adds triage evals with six right levels. Item 4 changes the doctor's checks of the clone. Item 5 makes the guardrails analyser name a refused glob. Item 6 moves credential commands to the moment a model is asked.

Claude Sonnet 5.5 built it. The first review ran at 6ca60149, after a restack of item 1 on step 11's compiler graph. Melian ran eleven rounds. Claude Sonnet 5.5 ran one review of record, at 735db267, with the gate and 34 mutation runs.

Reviewer letters: A is Codex. B to H are Melian's rounds one to seven. I is Claude. J, K, L and M are Melian's rounds eight to eleven. A finding's letter and number name it below.

Melian's output names a finding's rule, not its lens. Where this record names a lens, it reads the rule against the lens catalogue. Adjudication here is the record author's proposal and records the reported severity: P1 is high, P2 medium, P3 low. A maintainer may change any entry. Candidates do not create golden debt until a maintainer assigns it.

Three P1 policy notices (`package-lock.json`, `packages/evals/package.json`, `packages/evals/tsconfig.build.json`) and three P2 ones (`AGENTS.md`, `melian.yaml`, `packages/evals/triage/melian.yaml`) recur in every round.
