## Patterns

The notes record three. Each one shows a kind of defect the rounds kept finding one at a time.

- **The design lens held the as-built decision to the superseded one, until a `Supersedes:` line was added.** `0e0bca74` (round one), `8b6de05d` (round two) and `097b6605` (round four) each read the new decision against the base decision and found a clause the new one dropped or softened. Round three raised none, and the finding came back in round four. The fix that ended it was the coordinator's ruling and `4d515852`, which made the as-built decision supersede the first and restate every surviving clause. From round five the lens raised nothing on the decision. The earlier pull requests ([#111](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/111) B4 and C7, [#116](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/116) C8) found the same shape, so a decision that supersedes another and drops clauses has now repeated on three pull requests. It is a candidate check for the maintainer.
- **Each round found one more input missing from the adjudication task's attachment key.** `7735de28` (the per-check mapping) and `545e30f4` (lens defaults) in round one; `3889fa4a` in round two (the policy the task rereads at adjudication); `fd252e07` in round four (`ruleAliases` in the fingerprint, untested). Each was fixed by adding the input, and the next round found another. The step added a policy fingerprint, `f1785f73`, and `e6858e3b` has the task refuse a changed one. After round four the key stopped drawing findings. A key assembled input by input drifts whenever a new input arrives; round seven's `e356ded2` belongs to the same family, because the variant that reported a finding is also an input to its resolution.
- **After the retarget, three rounds each found one defect in how lens defaults and the policy notice resolve.** Round seven: a moved file takes the nearer variant's default (`e356ded2`). Round eight: the first fix listed only the variants above the path, so a move between sibling folders fell back to the destination's default (`13ac1769`). Round nine: a head can weaken a blocking notice by editing any resolution key, because the changed-resolution notice resolves through the table alone (`fd91445d`). The `trust-boundary` lens found the last, and the verifier lowered it from P1 to P2 with a stated reason: the shipped defaults give the same result, and only a base that raised the notice through a check entry was exposed. That is step 17's lowering working on its own repository. Round ten, the only round to see `a2bad5ff`, found no block. Each fix answered the finding it was given, and the next hole sat one case over: a lookup keyed by path and lens name, then by ancestors, then by table. `Lens.everywhere` now gives every variant the source defines. Lookups of this kind are worth a golden for the whole family.

The seven distinct blocks in rounds one to four were all about the decision or the attachment key. The three defects of weight in rounds seven to nine (two blocks and one lowered block) were all about variant and notice resolution. Neither A nor B found any of the three post-retarget defects.
