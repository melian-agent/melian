# Comparison review: pull request #31

Pull request: [melian-agent/melian#31](https://github.com/melian-agent/melian/pull/31), the plan that reshapes milestone 2 around Melian as the review of record for its own repository. It changes documentation only: the design, the plan, nine decision files, a progress-log entry, and four research notes.

Reviewers: Codex adversarial review, and an Opus review of record. Both reviewed the branch at 3065e38, and the fixes for both groups landed together afterwards, one commit per fix. Melian did not review this pull request: the change holds no code its lenses read, and the plan does not yet require Melian to review its own pull requests.

Adjudication gives the severity and the maintainer's disposition: fixed, a maintainer decision, qualified, or noise. No finding here becomes a golden. Each is about design documents, and no lens reviews documentation, so a golden would test nothing Melian runs.

## Differences

Codex found five, each a way the plan would let milestone 2 trust something it should not: a forgeable status, a policy a missing credential can weaken, a coverage report that proves less than claimed, a gate switched on before the defect that blocks it is fixed, and a record held in one clone. Two of its fixes were declined by decision rather than adopted: the gate rests on trusting writers until milestone 3, and a lost clone fails closed rather than sharing state early.

The Opus review of record found twenty-one, seventeen of its own. It read the documents against each other and against Enola's sources, so most of its findings are contradictions, undefined cases, and claims that outran their evidence. One was at the trust boundary and Codex missed it: Enola's `providers:` block runs an executable the repository names. Its findings drove three maintainer decisions, on unverified findings, the deduper, and escalation.

Four findings overlap: A1 and B20, A3 and B2, A4 and B3, and A5 and B21. A2 is wider than any one Opus finding; B8, B12, and B13 each fix part of it, and its central recommendation, that an outside-policy run never yields a verdict, was not taken. Neither reviewer raised a finding the maintainer judged noise.
