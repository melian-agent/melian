## Differences

The branch's own reviewer found eighteen. Its two highs were inside Melian's own bookkeeping: the configuration cache that a head could poison by turning a directory into a file, and the rename that made every old static result block. Its two-million-pattern differential fuzz of the engine against `RegExp` found one class of mismatch, `.` on line terminators, and nothing else.

Codex found five, all where the head under review reaches into the machinery that judges it: the critical head-supplied binary that runs before any analysis, the long line that fails open, and the analyser configuration a head can switch off. Two of its findings changed rules rather than code: an analyser configuration change now blocks, and what forbidden-patterns cannot scan is a finding, not a note.

The Opus review of record found twenty-two. Its own were about what a real monorepo does: workspace `node_modules` links that resolve to the checkout's sources, a solution-style `tsconfig.json` that checks nothing, and a rename that moves a file into a rule's scope. Its second fuzz agreed that the engine is sound.

Eleven findings overlap across the three groups. No reviewer found a defect on the crash path, because each check commits its findings and its status together.

Each valid finding marked as golden is to be written for the evals corpus once step 5 of the [implementation plan](../../../docs/design-implementation-plan.md) defines the format.
