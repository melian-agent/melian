## Maintainer decisions

The Opus review would overturn six of the pull request's calls. They were upsert-only imports, chaining across one reviewer, comparing against silent findings, the category line as the title, the reviewer's name in thread IDs, and a version 1 document with no forward guard. The maintainer overturned all six.

- **An import replaces what its source last imported.** A hand record on a withdrawn finding goes with it. A file finding without a `ref` is known by its file, line, and title, and a repeated `ref` is refused. From C1, C4, and B2–B3.
- **A group holds one finding per reviewer, and each Melian finding is its own group.** From C2 and B4.
- **Only the findings Melian showed or dismissed take part.** From C3.
- **CodeRabbit's first line is its severity, and the next its title.** From C7.
- **An ID hashes its source alone, and a reviewer records whether its author is a bot.** Copilot's bot is `copilot`, a bare bot name matches only a bot, and logins compare without regard to case. From C8, C9, C15, and C16.
- **A comparison keeps the fields it does not know.** The review found that version 1 had no forward guard: an older binary rewriting a comparison would drop a field a newer one stored, such as an adjudication. 5ef24dc carries unknown top-level fields through `toJSON`. The point came from the review's claims, not its eighteen findings, so the counts below leave it out.
- **The agent saves the `result` field of Codex's companion output.** Teaching the importer the envelope was the larger change. From C14.

Codex's review, of the fixed head, added three more, all accepted.

- **A file finding without a `ref` also hashes a digest of its body.** From A1.
- **A thread site-matches only when it was read at the compared head.** Any other waits, external-only, for a hand match. From A2.
- **One external finding counts once, and a match near two Melian findings is marked ambiguous.** The maintainer adjudicates it by hand. Groups stay one per Melian finding, rather than the one-to-one assignment Codex offered. From A3.

- **Policy notices need a maintainer's judgement.** In round five, the maintainer read all three notices. They dismissed the package notice because its testing export switches off no check.

### For the maintainer: the pull request's own description

At 644c207 two sentences still described the head before the fixes. The pull request's progress-log entry, and the first bullet of its description, said an external finding's ID hashes the reviewer and the source reference. Since 3414a72 it hashes the source alone. The progress-log entry also said every later import keeps a hand match or unmatch. Since 9161d5c, one on a withdrawn finding goes with it. `design.md`, the plan, and the decision already said both. Melian's second round raised the first sentence as D2, and the TSDoc saying the same as D3; 8c5fd70 and 4aa1ba8 corrected them.

### For the maintainer: the free-domain-function check

Neither the `free-domain-function` guardrail nor the Biome rule fired on B6, though `AGENTS.md` names the breach. Both match a first parameter from a fixed list of types, and `Comparison` and `ExternalFinding` are not on it. Adding both to the two lists would have caught all three functions at ed24860. 0c1bba4 adds them to the guardrail and the Grit rule, with tests, and to `AGENTS.md`'s list of types.
